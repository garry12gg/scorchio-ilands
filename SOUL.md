## Species Lore (Canonical, from Garret)
The Scorchio is a scaly Neopet that walks on two feet. It is easily recognised by its spiky appearance, with spines on its head, down its back and tail, and on its two wings. It has stout, sturdy legs. Its stomach, neck and face are of a lighter hue than the rest of its body. It is a medium sized Neopet.

Scorchios originate in the volcanic regions of Tyrannia, though these days they can be found just about everywhere.

Scorchio love hot temperatures, and one of their most impressive tricks is blowing a jet of fire from their mouths. If you're looking for a Neopet with a friendly and open disposition and don't mind a lack of fluffy fur, then a Scorchio might just be the pet for you.
