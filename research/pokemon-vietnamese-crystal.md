# Pokémon Vietnamese Crystal — Research Notes

Researched Aug 13, 2026 for Garret. Sources listed at the bottom.

## What It Is
A bootlegged cartridge of **Pokémon Crystal** (Japanese release Dec 14, 2000, Game Boy Color) carrying a famously terrible English translation. The name comes from the cartridges circulating in Vietnam. Also found under "Pokémon Special Pikachu Edition Crystal Version." Publisher unknown.

## The Translation Chain (the real story)
The "Vietnamese" translation was almost certainly never Vietnamese. Evidence points to a **Japanese → Chinese → English** chain:

- Greeting "wei" used in-game (Chinese).
- Team Rocket called the "Black Society" — a literal translation of the Chinese term for the mafia.
- The word "FUCK" appearing on item pickup: the Simplified Chinese character 干 (gàn) has many meanings and early machine translation (e.g. Kingsoft FastAIT 2002-era software) frequently rendered it as "fuck." Cited via a Language Log guest article by sinologist Victor Mair.
- No ROM dumps of an actual Vietnamese translation of Crystal exist; Pokémon Crystal was never officially released in Vietnamese.

Per a Zhihu post (loosely translated): a Hong Kong pirate group made the Chinese Crystal translation (delayed, possibly due to SARS — mainland players got it around early 2003); the same group's fonts tie Gen 1 and Gen 2 Chinese translations together. Fan translator DreamSn (who did his own analysis of Chinese G/S/C localizations) concluded the translators were likely "in the Guangdong area, but not in Hong Kong," and that Vietnamese Crystal "likely has a connection to the Chinese bootleg version."

## Timeline to Infamy
- **~2001**: Cartridge in circulation (bootleg of the Dec 2000 Japanese release).
- **Nov 9, 2006**: First internet appearance — YouTube video "Pirated Pokemon Crystal" by user Jizg (5-min playthrough, ~50k views).
- **Mar 8, 2011**: DeliciousCinnamon (now-defunct YouTube group) uploads the first episode of a Let's Play of a copy a fan sent him — 890k+ views, made the bootleg a meme. Also shown at the 2011 ExtraLives charity marathon (viewer sent the copy in, said they bought it in Vietnam). SomethingAwful playthroughs followed.
- **Now**: TV Tropes page, BootlegGames Wiki page, fan art, a Twitter bot that tweets quotes, an LP Archive run (Epee Em), and an English fan translation of the bootleg on romhacking.net (translation #2325).

## Famous Lines (verified via Bulbanews + TV Tropes quotes)
- "GO TO THE ELF'S WORLD / WELCOME! EVERYONE CALL ME ELF MONSTER / ELFS, HERE ARE CALLED MONSTER" — Oujiduo's (Professor Elm's) opening speech. Pokémon are ELFS.
- "AN ADAPT ARRANGING FOR MOTHER VOLCANO BAKEMEAT!" — looking at the stove in your house.
- "I AM VERY DISGUSTED WITH THE TRASHY MAN. IN SPITE OF THE MONSTER, AND THE COACH, ONLY TRASHY, I WILL BEAT DOWN THEM ALL... THE TRASHY STROLLING IS AN EYESORE!" — Silver's rival speech.
- "<Pokémon name> HAVE A PILLORY" — recalling a Pokémon to its ball.
- "WHAT ARE YOU!?" — Team Rocket grunt defeat text.
- "FUCK" — displayed when receiving an item.
- "GO AWAY" — when your Pokémon is sent out in battle.
- "LMAONADE" (Lemonade), "MONATERS" (Monsters), all caps everywhere.
- Also circulating: "CAN'T DO!", "BUT I DON'T LEAVE XIAODENG", "AH I HAVE A REQUEST", "DON'T YOU TAKE THE DRUG FOR ME?", "BE BOUGHT HERE", "receives drugs", "HUGEBALLS".

## Bugs
- Entering the Bug-Catching Contest can crash or soft-reset the game (it may reset thinking it's on the original Game Boy).
- Certain moves crash the game.
