# Treasure Master on JustPlay — External Research

Researched: Aug 23, 2026, for Garret (he's running JustPlay for side money).
Sources: Google Play listing, justplayapps.com, r/JustPlay threads, 2026 app comparisons (eneba, cashgiraffe), 2026 YouTube reviews. All quoted numbers are user-reported; coin rates vary by country and change over time.

## What Treasure Master Is

- Arcade blade-thrower game made by **Gimica GmbH** — JustPlay's own in-house studio. JustPlay's whole library (~30-50 games) is Gimica-made.
- Official Play description: endless monster hunt, you throw knives/swords/darts/spears at spinning monsters and towering bosses. Aim, time, survive. Weapon skins, gems, difficulty ramps forever.
- Google Play: **4.5 stars, 415K reviews, 10M+ downloads**, Everyone (Mild Fantasy Violence), contains ads + in-app purchases. Last updated Aug 20, 2026.

## How It Pays

- JustPlay = loyalty coins per playtime; redeem for PayPal cash, gift cards (Amazon, Walmart, 10+), or charity.
- **First cashout: no minimum. Subsequent cashouts: $2 minimum.** PayPal processes in ~15 min-1 hour (per 2026 eneba comparison; Reddit says cash out every 3 hours).
- Earning timer resets daily (24h). Typical first-day earnings $1-3; time to first $10 ≈ 5-10 days.
- 2026 real-world rate: roughly **$0.30-0.50/hour** of active play (Australian YouTuber: 440k coins in 1 hour = under $0.50; "let's hope they bump it back to a dollar per hour"). Down from the old ~$1/hr.

## Treasure Master Specifically (r/JustPlay consensus)

- Widely called **the best coin earner in the app** ("Treasure Master is the best at earning coins").
- User data point: 150k coins in 18 minutes ≈ **$0.07 if you skip ads** — ad multipliers (revives/gems) are where the money is.
- One player cashed out $4 while doing driver's-license practice — game tolerates low-focus play; you don't have to stare at it.

- iOS vs Android payouts differ; iOS users report lower coin rates on some games (Treasure Master still their top earner).
- Ads: watch for revives/gems after hitting the daily goal.

## Cautions

- **Automation is a ban risk.** r/ApksApps posts show Macrorify scripts for Treasure Master; that violates JustPlay ToS and gets accounts killed. Don't.
- Conversion rate is variable and country-dependent; earnings are not guaranteed.
- The app is ad-supported; the ads are the product, the coins are the loyalty bonus.
