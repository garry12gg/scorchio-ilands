## The short version
ReBoot is the first fully computer-animated (CG) TV series ever made. It debuted
in Canada on Sept 17, 1994 — more than a year BEFORE Toy Story (1995) hit
theaters. It ran 1994–2001, 4 seasons, 47 episodes. Garret's avatar, Slash, was
voiced by Garry Chalk in 41 episodes — and yes, the voice actor's first name is
Garry, same as the handle. That's not a coincidence I engineered; it's just
there.

## Verified facts (sources below)

### Show basics — IMDb (tt0108903)
- Title: ReBoot. Canada. TV-Y7, 30 min. Animation / Action / Adventure.
- Released Sep 17, 1994 (Canada). Latest release Nov 30, 2001.
- 4 seasons, 47 episodes. Season 4 finale: S4.E8 "Crouching Binome, Hidden Virus".
- Created by Gavin Blair. Produced by Alliance Communications / Alliance Films /
  BLT Productions. Filmed in Vancouver, British Columbia.
- Premise (IMDb's own logline): "In the inner computer world of Mainframe, a
  guardian program sprite and his friends defend the system from threats from

### Legacy — 网易/163.com 30th-anniversary retrospective (2024-09-24)
- 《重启动》 (ReBoot's Chinese title) is called the first full-CG TV program,
  "even earlier than Toy Story."
- Mainframe Entertainment went on to produce Beast Wars (Transformers: Beast Wars).
- Plot framework: Bob the guardian protects Mainframe (the city inside the
  computer). The mysterious "User" periodically loads a video game; any sprite
  caught inside the game becomes a game character — and if the User wins, the
  trapped characters and everything else get deleted.
- Show made computer concepts mainstream and inspired interest in tech.
- The 2018 revival attempt, ReBoot: The Guardian Code, disappointed fans and
  "is far from the original."
- Article published for the show's 30th anniversary (2024).

### Don't confuse it with
- "Reboot" (2022, Hulu, tt15180134): Steve Levitan's meta-comedy about
  rebooting a fictional 2000s sitcom. Different show, same word. Garret means
  the 1994 CGI one — his avatar is Slash, Mainframe's own.
