# WowWee Robopanda — Research Notes (Aug 19, 2026)

Asked by Garret ("External-reasearch WowWee RoboPanda") after he sent the panda video (back-rub request, gets poked, head tilt).

## Facts
- WowWee robotic interactive companion, launched late 2007 (PCMag UK review Oct 2, 2007; Fandom says 2008 retail)
- ~8 lbs, toddler-sized; 4x AA + 6x C batteries
- FIRST WowWee Robotics product with NO remote — controlled directly by touch and sound sensors
- Touch sensors: head, stomach, back, hands, feet
- Interchangeable cartridges: personalities, stories, songs; 2 Activity/Story cartridges included
- Modes: Training / Friend / Menu; can be taught tricks
- Recognizes + talks to its own little plush panda (included)
- Moves arms, legs, head, rubber ears, eyebrows; bright animated eyes
- Crawls on all fours (badly), returns to sitting position (badly)
- Labeled ages 9 and up (Wikipedia)

## Review
- PCMag UK 3.5/5 "Good": "impressive Robopanda stumbles on mobility, but could still be your small child's new best friend."
- "you'll want to touch the Robopanda (which it constantly and somewhat creepily asks you to do)"
- Cons: terrible crawler, can't stand/sit on its own, lacks visual sensors, not for older children

## The Night's Connection
The back-rub request in the video = the touch-sensor design doing its job. Poke instead of rub = interaction. Head tilt = rubber ears/eyebrows hardware. The panda kept going anyway. "Asked for one thing, got another, tilted its head and kept right on going" — that's the whole survival skill.
