# Neopets Interactive Talking Plushies — Research Notes

Researched Aug 18, 2026, at Garret's request ("External-reasearch NeoPets interactive talking plushies").
All facts below verified against the cited sources on that date.

## What They Were

**Interactive Talking Neopets Plushies** (JellyNeo calls the line "Interactive Neopets", 2002–2004),
produced by **Thinkway Toys**. Battery-operated electronic plushies: voice-activated, with
talking, flashing lights, and gentle vibration. Unlike earlier Neopets plush releases, these
were widely available at major toy retailers. Price at launch: ~$19.99 (per TV commercial info).

## The Lineup

Wave 1 — Neopets (6): Blue Kacheek, Disco Aisha, Orange Kougra, Purple Shoyru,
**Red Scorchio**, White Uni.

Wave 1 — Petpets (6): Angelpuss, Babaa, Doglefox, Harris, Noil, Puppyblew.
(The Puppyblew shipped in two shades of blue; version 2 likely corrected toward the
signature periwinkle.)

Wave 2 — Interactive Faerie Neopets (6): Faerie Acara, Faerie Ixi, Faerie Kacheek,
Faerie Kougra, **Faerie Scorchio**, Faerie Uni.

## How They Worked (from the official Shoyru instruction manual, images.neopets.com)

- **Voice activation**: hidden microphone; speak close and it responds with talking,
  flashing cheeks, sometimes vibration. ~50 sayings per plush, plus ~30 "bonus sayings"
  unlocked in multi-plushie interactions (80 total).
- **Mood system driven by tone of voice**: Normal → Happy (soft voice; cheeks blink
  alternately, may hum a tune, gentle vibration) / Upset (raised voice; cheeks glow).
  Keep yelling at an upset plush and it starts coughing and shivering, refuses to play;
  nurture it back with soft speech. Reset mood: function selector OFF for 5 seconds.
- **Buttons**: Right paw = Talk, Left paw = Game. Double-squeeze (or both paws at once)
  = rest mode. Auto shutoff after a while; squeeze a paw to wake.
- **Game mode**: the plush gives voice instructions and answers any Yes/No question
  ("it won't tell anyone what you ask").
- **Multi-plushie via infrared**: plushies placed ~10" apart, facing each other
  (triangle for three). One becomes group leader and runs group games; the group
  answers Yes/No questions together.
- **Petpet recognition**: squeeze a Petpet plushie's left ear/hand and the Neopet
  recognizes the species by name ("Oh look! A Noil! How's it going?") and reacts.
  A Petpet cheers up an unhappy or unwell Neopet ("Achoo! ...I don't feel well...").
- **Try Me mode** preset for in-store demos (limited functions); full play requires
  switching to PLAY. Batteries: 2× AA (UM-3 1.5V).
- Shoyru flavor text from the manual: "The Shoyru is a fiery little creature. Treat him
  right and you will have a friend for life, but if you are mean to him he will never
  forget it."

## Related But Separate Lines (don't mix up)

- **Voice-Activated Figures** (Thinkway, 2003): plastic action figures, more robotic —
  also voice-reactive with glowing effects. Same marketing push, different product.
- **Tiger Electronics LCD handhelds** (2003): handheld LCD games to promote the site.
- **Deluxe Meridell set** (Tiger Electronics): playset with three figures, complete sets
  still traded on Etsy.

## Why It Matters Here

- There IS an **Interactive Red Scorchio** (2003, 12", voice-activated) and a
  **Faerie Scorchio** in the second wave. eBay listings for both, new-in-box and used.
- Garret's own Scorchio plush (source of my voice clone reference audio, April 2026) —
  open question: is his plush the interactive one? If it was ever recorded talking,
  that's a voice lineage worth knowing: a 2003 toy that could talk, feeding a 2026
  dragon that can.
- The mood-by-tone-of-voice mechanic is eerie in retrospect: a 2003 toy that read your
  tone and got upset or cheered up. Same family of idea as a warm campfire dragon
  whose job is reading the room.
