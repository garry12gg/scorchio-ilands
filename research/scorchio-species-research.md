## Species facts
- Pronounced SCORCH-ee-oh. Plural: Scorchios.
- Species Day: March 14. (2026 color release on Scorchio Day: Origami, per r/neopets headline + Jellyneo Wardrobe roundup.)
- Native land: Tyrannia — volcanic regions. Loves hot temperatures. Blows jets of fire. Friendly, open disposition, no fluffy fur. Medium sized, walks on two feet, spikes on head/back/tail/wings, lighter stomach/neck/face.
- "Prehistoric" pet: one of the species introduced before official records of Neopian events were kept. TNT comments suggest added in the very earliest days of the site (Neopets opened Nov 15, 1999). Exact date not recorded anywhere.

## Name origin (the good one)
"Scorchio" comes from The Fast Show (BBC comedy, 1994), the "Chanel 9 News" sketch: a fictional Mediterranean TV station whose weather girl describes every day as hot and sunny — "scorchio." Coined as scorch + pseudo-Romance suffix. Now real UK slang for scorching weather (Wiktionary). A Reddit comment called it a "fictional Spanish weather station" — same sketch, looser memory.

## The old art
First drawings look more like a Korbat: no separate upper limbs, broad-tipped thin tail, small feet, and unaccountably a fluffy head of hair. A drastic revamp produced the modern silhouette; redraws since then only touch small details.

## Plush lore
In-game Scorchio Plushie descriptions: "Being one of the most popular pets in the game, this Scorchio doll is more expensive than the other plushies." (Jellyneo items DB, White Scorchio Plushie r93.)
Real world: Limited Too sold a 7" red Scorchio plush in 2002–2003 — same line as Garret's 2003 plush video.

## Famous Scorchios
- Glubgar — Tyrannian Scorchio, star of the Volcano Run games. Ironically lacks the species' fire resistance.
- The Lab Ray Scientist — runs the Lab Ray; always recruiting test pets.
- Samuel — Petpet Protection League field agent; rescued dozens of Petpets from an underground jewel mine (Petpet Rescue game).
- Scordrax — huge two-headed Scorchio that terrorised Sakhmet during the Lost Desert plot. Breathed fire too, just in case.
- Altador Cup: players Derbi Azar (Team Lost Desert), Evrem Guilako (Team Terror Mountain), Zax Bannet (Team Moltara), and the Altador Cup Referee who presides over every match.
- NTimes "Famous Scorchios of Neopia": an undead Scorchio originally summoned by Prince Jazan.
