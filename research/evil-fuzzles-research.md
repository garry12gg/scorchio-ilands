# Evil Fuzzles from Beyond the Stars — Research Notes

Researched 2026-08-13 (requested by Garret). Sources: Jellyneo, The Daily Neopets, Neopets Wiki (Fandom API), Neopets.com game pages, Neopets Classic, r/neopets.

## The Game

- Retired Neopets game, Virtupets Space Station world. Currently in the Game Graveyard.
- **Original: game ID 128**, released **March 30, 2002**. Original SWF still hosted: `http://images.neopets.com/games/g128_graveyard.swf` (playable via the tombstone in the Game Graveyard).
- **Remake: game ID 585** — the Neopets team gave it a makeover at some point; this is the version on the current game page (`neopets.com/games/game.phtml?game_id=585`). Old version lives in the graveyard.
- Also playable today on **Neopets Classic** (community revival): `neopetsclassic.com/games/game?game_id=585`.
- Trophy game (high scores). 10,000,000-plays milestone on the game page.
- NP ratio: 1.20 points = 1 NP (600 pts = 500 NP, 1,200 pts = 1,000 NP).

## Premise

Evil Fuzzles attack the Virtupets Space Station. You play **Zygorax** (Jellyneo) / **Xygorax** (Daily Neopets) driving the **X-Scrub 4000 Walker Bot**, travelling station to station, zapping mutated Fuzzles with laser cannons (mouse aim, left-click fire) and clearing mines. Spacebar drops a Smart Bomb.

## Gameplay Notes

- Fuzzles spawn in pairs; the next pair doesn't spawn until the current pair dies (r/neopets).
- Fuzzle toughness tiers by color (basic gun): Red 1 shot (levels 1-2), Yellow 2 (2-3), Green 3 (3-4), Brown 4 (4-5), Blue 5 (5-6), Purple 6 (6-7), Grey 7+ (2 shots even at max gun).
- Mega Gun upgrades levels 1-6. Each upgrade level subtracts one shot per Fuzzle.
- Station purchases with **Virtucreds** (earned per hit alongside points): Hull (+10), Shields (withstand 4 mine explosions), Droid Repair (auto-heals 5 hull/level), Radar (warns of side attackers), Mega Gun, Smart Bomb (clear screen), Stun Bomb. Max 5 of each bomb.
- Chain/combo system: consecutive kills without missing build a chain; a bonus sign appears on screen, shoot it to cash in. 2 pts per Fuzzle up to 9 kills, 3 pts per Fuzzle at 10+ kills. Killing 10 without collecting upgrades the multiplier from x2 to x3 (r/neopets). Shooting mines does NOT break the chain; missing a Fuzzle does.
- Strategy consensus: target Fuzzles before mines; collect bonuses at x3; two upgrade schools (max gun vs. one gun upgrade + repair droids).
- Trivia: Zygorax's vehicle moves at 72 km/h, accelerates to 120 km/h at the "Approaching Station" stage (fan-measured).

## The Fuzzle (the creature)

- A Fuzzle is a **toy** — a fluffy plushie with a giant grin, sold at the **Plushie Palace**. Regular and Evil versions exist.
- 50+ fuzzle items. Colors: Black, Blue, Brown, Cloud, Dastardly, Electric, Garlic, Ghost, Gold, Green, Grey, Insane, Mutant, Orange, Pink, Purple, Rainbow, Royal, Spotted, Starry, Striped, Tyrannian, White, Yellow — many with "evil" variants.
- Fuzzles can break if you play with them too much. (Handle with care.)
- **Avatar**: play with a *Blue Evil Fuzzle* until it scares your pet and says BOO! → "Evil Fuzzle – BOO!" avatar.
- Cooking Pot recipe: Flaming Torch + Purple Fuzzle = **Flaming Wuzzle Toy**.
- Neopets help pages: "What is the difference between a Fuzzle and an Evil Fuzzle?" and "Is a Fuzzle really a baby JubJub?" (lore rabbit holes).
- Neopian Times editorial: "Not as Cute as You'd Think, By a Concerned Neopet" (issue 299).
