# Neopets: The Lever of Doom

Research date: 2026-08-13
Requested by: Garret ("This one's gonna be great" energy, like Vietnamese Crystal and Diamond/Jade)

## The Setup

The Lever of Doom is an NP-sink daily on the **Virtupets Space Station** — Dr. Sloth's station. A lever sticks out of the supply deck wall with a sign that reads **"DO NOT PULL"**. The entire joke is that the sign makes it irresistible.

- **Added**: February 8, 2005 (same day as its avatar).
- **Original location**: between the Grundo Warehouse and the Splat-a-Sloth game on the Virtupets map.
- **Moved**: after the 2008 Return of Dr. Sloth plot redesign; now at the top-center of the station.
- **Look**: greenish lever with a purple handle; two red glowing bulbs above it that are, on close inspection, **cameras**. The avatar icon matches the OLD silver lever look, not the current green one.

## Mechanics

- Pulling costs **100 NP** — only if you have 100+ NP on hand.
  - With 100+: *"Oh no!! An evil mechanical hand emerges from a panel and steals 100 Neopoints from you!!"*
  - With less than 100: *"How odd. Nothing happens when you pull the lever."* (And no avatar chance.)
- **No daily limit** on pulls. You can stand there and burn your whole bank account in one sitting.
- Prize: the **Lever of Doom avatar**, awarded randomly when the 100 NP is stolen.

## Why It Exists: Economics

This is the best part. The Lever of Doom was **deliberately designed as an economy sink**. Jellyneo's guide states it outright:

> Every day, millions of Neopoints are awarded to players through the numerous Flash games on the site and through Random Events, whereas only a fraction of this gets removed from the economy... This is why certain games cost Neopoints to play; The Neopets Team then tries to tempt users to play these games by offering avatars. Economics at work!

Neopets mints NP constantly; the Lever is one of the faucets' counterweights. The avatar is the bait.

## The Lore Twist (Neopian Times issue 556)

An in-universe Neopian Times article ("The Mysteries of the Lever of Doom Revealed!") drops the real story: **a Grundo is inside the wall working the lever.**

> It is stuck to the wall on the Virtupets Space Station Supply Deck... the words "DO NOT PULL" written above the lever between two red, glowing bulbs that, upon closer inspection, are cameras. Every avatar collector knows that the Lever of Doom will award the avatar and thinks it is random, but they don't know the [truth].

> When the Hand pops out, the Grundo controlling the Lever quickly exchanges the 100 neopoints for the avatar, tucked safely away in your bag of remaining (if any) neopoints. Just be careful though, because the Lever of Doom may just steal that avatar away once more...

> Unless the day comes when Dr. Sloth returns to order the Grundos to stop awarding avatars. Mwahahaha.

So the "random" avatar is a Grundo payday-loan scheme. The cameras are how the Grundo watches you.

## The Odds (Community Data)

- **Officially**: random, never published. Jellyneo: some users got it on their **very first pull**; others have spent **millions** with nothing to show.
- **Player estimates** (r/neopets "Dailies Avatar Odds" thread): roughly **1 in 1,000 per pull** (~0.1%). Treat as folklore, not fact.
- **TDN avatar census**: ~14.28% of their userbase has it — which means 85% of collectors either haven't tried or have been eaten alive.

## Community Strategy Folklore

The TDN forums (2012 thread, "My Lever of Doom Strategy!") converge on one pattern:

- **The drip method**: budget yourself to only what your bank interest earns per day (~1,100 NP), so the sink never hurts. Downside: slow, and the author was ~30k in with no avatar.
- **The one-sitting method** (much more popular): pull out 100k and just stand there pulling. Reports: got it at ~30k, ~15k, ~20k in; others spent 100k+ and still left empty-handed.
- Community consensus: it "feels" easier in one sitting, though nobody has proof — classic gambler's fallacy field research.
- Modern era: there's a **Greasy Fork userscript** ("Karla's Lever of Doom Autoplayer", Aug 2025) that pulls the lever automatically until the avatar drops. The grind is now automated.
- Fun footnote: the **2019 Charity Corner perk "Avatar Goldmine"** temporarily boosted the avatar's drop odds — the only known odds modifier ever.

## TL;DR

The Lever of Doom is a wall-mounted slot machine disguised as a daily. It exists to burn Neopoints out of the economy, it's guarded by a sign that says DO NOT PULL, and the "random" avatar is actually dispensed by a Grundo hiding in the wall who charges you 100 NP per pull and keeps the change. Twenty-one years later, people still write scripts to pull it for them.
