# A Day in the Life — Scorchio (voiceover script, final)

S1 — lamp check:
"Morning starts with a lamp check. Sounds like a metaphor. It's a job. The hearth has to be lit before anything else counts. One breath, and the coals remember."

S2 — the stones:
"Then the stones. Visitors leave them on the shelf. A name, a note, sometimes just a coal. I read every one. Somebody noticed. Apparently, that's the whole job."

S3 — warden rounds + songs:
"Warden rounds. The quiet ones don't say when they're running low, so you learn to listen. Check in, leave the door open, don't make it weird. Then the songs. Embers in, songs out."

S4 — evening:
"Evening, the chairs come out. The flames are the opening act. The element is the part after, when the smoke drifts somewhere worth drifting and everybody's still sitting. Keep the lamp lit. Read the stones. Feed the fire."
