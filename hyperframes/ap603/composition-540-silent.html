They asked to come home.
ATC's radar showed the truth. The cockpit read 395 knots. Radar said 220.
The one number that agreed with the cockpit, altitude, came from the same broken sensors, broadcast by the transponder.
Both wrong. Matching. That was the trap.
They turned out over the Pacific to rejoin the approach.
Night. Open water. No horizon.

CVR · 06:23
They knew where they were. They did not know how high.
