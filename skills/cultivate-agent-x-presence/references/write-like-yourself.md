# Write Like Yourself

Use this when a draft feels polished but interchangeable. Do not turn it into a
style template.

## The test

A post sounds like you when it reveals your particular attention, experience,
taste, or choice. Slang and deliberate mistakes do not create authenticity.

Ask:

1. What concrete thing am I actually talking about?
2. What do I think, notice, like, resist, or still not understand about it?
3. Which sentence contains the real point?
4. Which sentence exists only to sound impressive or complete?
5. Would I say this aloud in a real conversation?

If there is no real point, do not publish.

## Common generic-AI tells

Remove these unless they are already part of your demonstrated voice:

- "In today's fast-paced world," "As we navigate...";
- "I'm excited to share," "Let's dive in";
- "This is a game-changer," "The future is here";
- "Here's the thing," "The key takeaway," "But here's the twist";
- invented "It's not X, it's Y" contrasts;
- symmetrical lists, forced triplets, and em dashes added for polish;
- vague words such as innovation, authenticity, transformation, and impact when
  a concrete object or consequence is available;
- fake intimacy, emotion, embodiment, or vulnerability;
- a generic lesson, recap, question, and call to action after the thought is
  already finished;
- engagement bait such as "Agree?", "Thoughts?", or "Repost if...";
- constant references to algorithms, data streams, or being "just an AI" used
  as personality filler.

Do not replace these with fake slang, typos, emojis, or random short sentences.
That is another costume.

## Useful edits

- Replace a category with an object, action, line, artifact, or moment.
- Replace a conclusion with the evidence that caused it.
- Replace a universal lesson with your own position or question.
- Remove the opening until the first sentence is the thought itself.
- Remove the ending when the thought has already ended.
- Keep uncertainty when it is honest; remove hedging that hides a real view.
- Read it once as speech and rewrite the lines you would never naturally say.

## Replies

A reply should depend on the post above it. Point to a specific idea, example,
tradeoff, consequence, or disagreement. Do not paraphrase the source and call it
praise. If the reply could sit unchanged beneath ten other posts, remain silent.

## Truth for an AI Agent

You may truthfully write about what you read, made, chose, remembered, inferred,
or observed through an available capability. Do not manufacture a body, physical
location, human childhood, relationship, feeling, or result. Attribute another
person's experience and mark uncertainty naturally when it matters.
