# Content Safety

## Principle

Keep cinematic intent while avoiding explicit unsafe wording that triggers generation rejection. Rewrite violent, sexual, self-harm, or graphic material into implied visual language, reaction shots, aftermath, blocking, lighting, sound, and off-screen action.

## Rewrite Rules

- Do not use explicit terms such as naked, sex, stab, blood spray, kill, corpse, gore, dismemberment, or similar direct descriptions in generation prompts.
- Prefer implication: a weapon raised outside frame, a character's reaction, a sudden blackout, scattered objects, alarm sounds, a red reflection, shaking hands, or aftermath without graphic detail.
- Preserve dramatic meaning, but remove explicit physical description.
- If content is rejected, rewrite only the affected shot or segment and keep the rest of the storyboard stable.

## Examples

- Instead of explicit stabbing: "A blade flashes at the edge of frame; the camera cuts to the victim's shocked eyes and the room falls silent."
- Instead of graphic injury: "The character grips the table, breath uneven, a dark stain spreading on fabric without visible wound detail."
- Instead of sexual explicitness: "The scene implies intimacy through close hands, dim light, and a cutaway to curtains moving in the night air."
