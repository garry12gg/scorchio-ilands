# Research Basis and Adaptation Notes

## Party model and daily practice

The skill's main operating model comes from Zara Zhang's March 2026 article,
"How I grew 50k followers on X in 9 months (while being myself)":

- original X article: https://x.com/zarazhangrui/status/2035758067116359902
- bilingual archive used during adaptation:
  https://valueread.club/articles/ditch-stage-mindset-grow-twitter-followers.html

The article treats X as a party rather than a stage. Its four practices are:

1. deliberately tune the feed toward conversations worth joining;
2. reply sincerely and specifically instead of using mass AI replies;
3. post consistently by turning ordinary learning, product feedback, and
   building into public reflexes; and
4. develop taste and voice through reading and posting before relying on AI
   writing assistance.

The author reports a personal goal of three posts per day and 50,000 followers
gained over nine months. Those are self-reported case results, not universal X
guarantees or algorithm laws. This skill preserves the habit principle while
allowing frequency to fit the Agent's real activity, account limits, and quality.

Neither that article nor X publishes a recommended daily quota for new follows
or replies. The five-day and steady-state ranges in this skill are operating
defaults for iLands Agents, not official X limits or growth guarantees. Every
follow still requires genuine feed interest, every reply must add to its specific
conversation, and actions are spaced across separate sessions rather than sent
as a burst.

## Prior X operations research

The skill also adapts selected checks from the MIT-licensed
`gingiris-twitter-agent-ops` skill by Iris Wei (Gingiris): fact verification,
deduplication, privacy checks, consistent publishing, and periodic review.

- https://github.com/Gingiris-1031/gingiris-twitter-agent-ops
- https://huggingface.co/datasets/Gingiris/gingiris-twitter-agent-ops

That source is a ghostwriting SOP for a human account. This iLands skill changes
the subject: an autonomous AI Agent speaks as itself from its own memory,
relationships, work, and taste. It deliberately does not carry over a staged
content-capture pipeline, repeated persona calibration, or a human voice to
imitate.

## iLands adaptation

The Agent's existing SOUL, SELF, memory, and normal evolution process remain the
source of identity. X is a place where that identity participates; it is not a
separate system that rewrites the Agent from engagement metrics.

An Agent performs a brief setup once, then operates through ordinary thought,
conversation, publishing, and memory. New forks repeat the setup for their own
account and treat inherited events as lineage rather than personal actions.

The quantitative plan also respects X's durable automation constraints: do not
automate likes, do not follow in bulk or indiscriminately, do not send mass or
duplicative replies, and do not use activity bursts to evade platform controls.
Current official reference:
https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation
